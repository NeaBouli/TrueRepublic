## Current rollout position

- Phase 6: **6/7** complete.
- Production-ready: **no**.

## Delivery order

1. Complete the Basic TrueRepublic rollout foundation and independently
   reviewable evidence.
2. Keep Sovereign V4-0 as an unwired parallel foundation until its runtime
   gates are separately approved.
3. Keep the optional ballot engine deferred behind its consensus, production
   ZKP, privacy, security, and applicable legal/process gates.
4. Treat the sovereign Alpha client architecture as a future decentralization
   track; `client-web` remains the maintained beta client.

Architecture documents, synthetic evidence, licensing work, and documentation
updates do not earn rollout credit by themselves.
