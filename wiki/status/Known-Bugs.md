# Known Bugs and Limitations

The maintained limitation register is
[`docs/LIMITATIONS.md`](https://github.com/NeaBouli/TrueRepublic/blob/main/docs/LIMITATIONS.md).
Open defects and work items are tracked in
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/NeaBouli/TrueRepublic/issues).

TrueRepublic is not production-ready. Do not use real keys, funds, anonymous
ballots, or public-network infrastructure until the relevant rollout,
security, privacy, and operations gates pass.
