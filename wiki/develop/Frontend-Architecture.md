# Frontend Architecture

`client-web` is the only maintained client. Its current architecture, commands,
wallet boundary, tests, and limitations are documented in the
[client README](https://github.com/NeaBouli/TrueRepublic/blob/main/client-web/README.md).

The former `web-wallet` and `mobile-wallet` prototypes were retired and
removed; they remain available only in Git history. A later sovereign client
is described separately in the Alpha architecture and does not replace the
maintained beta client today.
